How does 2D FFT actually work? · Advanced

The 2D DFT is separable: FFT every row, then every column. The inverse restores the image from both magnitude and phase.

F[u,v] = Σ_y Σ_x I[x,y] exp(−j2π(ux+vy)/N)
g[x,y] = IFFT2{H[u,v] F[u,v]}
g[x,y] = Σa Σb h[a,b] I[(x−a) mod N,(y−b) mod N]
